Product Overview: AI Marketing Platform  
The AI Marketing Platform consists of two independent modules, each solving a different business problem. 
Module 1: AI Marketing Agent 
Objective: Automate the complete marketing lifecycle from market research to content generation using 
multiple specialized AI agents. 
Marketing Discovery 
The platform supports two methods of initiating marketing workflows. 
A. Automated Market Monitoring 
Designed for continuous market intelligence. This enables businesses to receive marketing opportunities 
automatically without manually providing topics. 
• User Configuration: 
• RSS Feed Source(s) 
• Industry 
• Keywords 
• Categories 
• Monitoring Frequency (Daily / Weekly / Custom) 
Workflow: RSS Feed → Latest News Collection → Automatic Trend Identification → Research Agent → 
Competitive Intelligence → Remaining Marketing Workflow. 
B. Manual Topic Research 
Designed for targeted content creation. The provided topic initiates the complete AI Marketing workflow. 
• User Input: 
• Topic (e.g., Agentic AI) 
• Industry (e.g., Manufacturing) 
• Business Objective (e.g., Generate Leads) 
• Target Audience (e.g., CXOs) 
AI Agents 
1. Marketing Supervisor Agent 
Responsibilities: Workflow orchestration, agent execution, dependency management, final context 
coordination. 
2. Trend Identification Agent 
Responsibilities: Trending keywords, search trends, popular hashtags, frequently searched questions, 
seasonal opportunities. 
Output: Trend Report, Keyword Opportunities. 

=== PAGE 2 ===
3. Research Agent 
Responsibilities: Industry research, market reports, customer pain points, emerging technologies, business 
opportunities, latest news. 
Output: Research Summary, Business Insights. 
4. Competitive Intelligence Agent 
Responsibilities: Analyze competitors through Website, Blogs, LinkedIn, Case Studies, Advertisements, SEO 
Keywords, Messaging, Positioning. 
Output: Competitor Benchmark, Content Gap Analysis, Positioning Opportunities. 
5. Context Merger 
Combines outputs from Trend Identification, Research, and Competitive Intelligence. 
Produces: Unified Marketing Context. 
6. Content Strategy Agent 
Determines: Content Type, Marketing Objective, Audience, Communication Style, Distribution Channels. 
Possible outputs: Blog, LinkedIn, Newsletter, Webinar, Whitepaper, Video Script, Carousel, Case Study. 
7. Content Planning Agent 
Creates: Weekly Calendar, Monthly Calendar, Publishing Schedule, Platform Selection, Best Publishing 
Time. 
8. SEO Agent 
Optimizes: Primary Keywords, Secondary Keywords, Meta Title, Meta Description, Search Intent, Internal 
Linking, FAQ Suggestions. 
9. Content Generation Agent 
Generates: Blogs, LinkedIn Posts, Articles, Newsletters, Landing Pages, Email Campaigns, Whitepapers, 
Case Studies. 
10. Creative Generation Agent 
Generates: Images, Videos, Infographics, Marketing Creatives, Architecture Diagrams. 
Human Approval: Every generated asset enters an approval workflow before publishing. Future versions 
will support automated publishing. 
Module 2: Website Intelligence Agent 
Objective: Provide businesses with a comprehensive AI-powered website assessment by combining 
website analysis, business context understanding, gap identification, and intelligent recommendations. 
Unlike the Marketing Agent, this module evaluates an existing website against the business objectives and 
identifies opportunities for improvement. 

=== PAGE 3 ===
User Inputs 
1. 1. Business Information: 
• Business Name 
• Company Overview 
• Products 
• Services 
• Target Audience 
• Industry 
• Business Goals 
A dedicated Instruction & Example section will guide users on how to provide complete and accurate 
business context. 
2. 2. Website URL: 
• The primary domain to be analyzed (e.g., https://companyname.com) 
AI Agents 
1. Website Structure Agent 
Responsibilities: Crawl website, discover all pages, analyze website structure, navigation hierarchy, 
internal links, technical overview, page inventory. 
Output: Complete Website Structure Report. 
2. Business Gap Analysis Agent 
Uses Business Context and Website Content, comparing both to identify gaps. 
• Analysis Areas: 
• Business Alignment: Products covered, services covered, missing offerings, business messaging. 
• Content Analysis: Missing pages, weak content, missing blogs, case studies, news section, FAQs. 
• SEO Analysis: Keyword coverage, meta content, heading structure, content optimization. 
• Conversion Analysis: CTA visibility, contact pages, lead forms, trust indicators. 
• User Experience: Navigation, readability, content organization. 
Output: Comprehensive Gap Analysis. 
3. Recommendation & Scoring Agent 
Consolidates findings from all previous agents. 
• Generates: 
• Website Health Score (0–100) 
• Category-wise scores: Business Alignment, Technical Quality, SEO, Content Quality, User 
Experience, Conversion Readiness. 
• Recommendations: High, Medium, and Low Priority Improvements.

• Suggests: New pages to create, pages to remove, content to improve, blog/news strategy, service
